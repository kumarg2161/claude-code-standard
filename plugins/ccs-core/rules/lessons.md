# Lessons

Written by Claude when you correct a mistake it made. Format:

`- **[Category]**: <What went wrong> → <Correct approach>`

This file ships empty on purpose. Lessons are specific to the projects and
clients you work on, so they stay in your own copy rather than being shared.
Anything genuinely general belongs in `core-behaviors.md` or `code-standards.md`
instead, where it is reviewed rather than accumulated.

Note on the cap: CLAUDE.md instructs Claude to delete the oldest entry past 15.
That evicts by age, not by value. Graduate entries you want to keep into the
rules files above before they age out.
